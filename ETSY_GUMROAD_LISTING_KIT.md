## Title

Ark — Offline Household Handover & Printable Family Organizer

## Description

Make important household information easier for a trusted person to find when you are unavailable. Ark is a downloadable browser workspace with seven guided essentials, household notes, explicit review dates, and selected family or caregiver packets.

Record who to call, where documents live, which bills need attention, and the routines your household depends on. Notes begin as drafts; reviews are due after 90 days. The checklist is not a safety assessment. Set a trusted-person plan, print a packet, and practice finding information together.

No account, subscription, analytics, external fonts, remote scripts, or cloud sync. All runtime assets are included. **The working browser copy is unencrypted. Downloaded backups use passphrase encryption.** Protect your computer, keep separate backups, and test restoration. Forgotten passphrases cannot be recovered.

Packets, PDFs, and copied briefings are unencrypted and cannot be revoked. Inspect all selected information before sharing. Store document locations and authorized access instructions, not passwords, PINs, safe combinations, or full identity numbers.

## Compatibility and contents

Current desktop browsers. Python 3 recommended for consistent localhost storage; standalone HTML also available with browser-dependent file storage. Switching browser/profile/location/launch method may require backup/restore. No live synchronization or mobile app. Operating-system launch permissions, unzip steps, and clipboard restrictions can apply. Includes local app, launchers, quick-start guide, practice checklist, and privacy/security notes.

## Important limits

Ark does not create legal authority, generate legal documents, provide medical/financial/legal advice, notify institutions, or grant account access. It has not received an independent security audit or professional content certification. Browser data can be cleared or lost. Keep a tested backup and an accessible current printed packet.
