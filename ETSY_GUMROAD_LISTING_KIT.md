## ❓ 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) for Product Page

**Q: Where is my family's sensitive data stored?**  
A: Strictly on your own computer! Ark Vault uses your browser's private local storage. It never sends a single byte of data to our servers, the cloud, or any third party.

**Q: Do I need to pay a recurring monthly or annual fee?**  
A: No! This is a one-time digital purchase. You download it once, and it belongs to your household forever.

**Q: Do I need any coding or technical skills?**  
A: Absolutely not. It is designed for everyday parents. You simply double-click the launcher file and it opens in your web browser just like a website, but runs 100% offline.

**Q: Can I transfer my data to another computer or spouse's laptop?**  
A: Yes! Simply click "Backup / Restore" > "Download ark-backup.json". You can move this file onto a USB thumb drive and click "Restore from Backup" on any other device.

**Q: Can I print a physical copy for my home safe?**  
A: Yes! Click "Print Safe Binder" at any time. Ark automatically formats your records into a clean, black-and-white legal binder ready for printing.
