PRIVACY, IN PLAIN WORDS

Your information stays here.

Ark has no accounts, analytics, external fonts, cloud sync, or remote scripts. The application makes no outbound network requests.

Your browser copy

Notes are stored unencrypted in this browser profile on this device. Someone with access to that profile may read them. Device encryption and your screen lock are your responsibility.

Your backups

Downloaded backups use AES-256-GCM with a passphrase-derived key. Backups do not protect an unlocked browser or a compromised device. Keep a strong passphrase and give your trusted person a separate way to find it.

Your responsibility

Clearing browser data, private browsing, changing browser profiles, or moving the app can make records unavailable. Directly opened files have browser-dependent storage behavior. Keep and test a backup; print a current packet.

What Ark does

Ark organizes information and document locations. It does not create legal authority, give medical or financial advice, notify institutions, or grant access to accounts. Review legal and care instructions with a qualified professional. If there is immediate danger, contact local emergency services.
