# 🛡️ 72-Hour Household In-Case-of-Emergency (ICE) Checklist
### Companion Physical Audit Sheet for Fireproof Safes & Next-of-Kin

Keep this printed sheet in the front sleeve of your Ark Emergency Binder inside your fireproof safe.

---

## ⏱️ PHASE 1: HOURS 0 – 24 (Immediate Shock & Survival)
- [ ] **First Phone Call:** Alternate trustee / closest sibling notified.
- [ ] **Immediate Cash Reserve:** Minimum $2,000–$5,000 liquid physical cash in home safe for unexpected expenses (hotels, food, urgent travel).
- [ ] **Safe Combinations & Master Keys:** Documented in Ark Vault; backup key location entrusted to designated co-trustee.
- [ ] **Dependent Minor Children & Pets:** Immediate guardian notified; temporary care instructions handed over.
- [ ] **Employer HR Notification:** Call employer benefits department to freeze benefits, initiate group term life insurance claim, and verify final payroll draft.

---

## ⏱️ PHASE 2: DAYS 2 – 7 (Financial & Domestic Continuity)
- [ ] **Primary Life Insurance Policies:** Policy numbers, claim phone lines, and death certificate requirement list accessed.
- [ ] **Mortgage & Escrow Continuity:** Auto-draft bank account verified and funded to prevent late fees or credit damage.
- [ ] **Primary Checking & Payroll:** Joint account access confirmed; ensure automatic drafts for utilities (power, water, internet) remain active.
- [ ] **Digital Identity & Master Password Vault:** 1Password / Bitwarden emergency sheet and Apple ID Legacy Contact initiated.
- [ ] **Mail & Package Forwarding:** USPS mail hold or key access arranged.

---

## ⏱️ PHASE 3: DAYS 8 – 30 (Legal, Estate & Long-Term Transition)
- [ ] **Living Trust & Will Execution:** Original signed and notarized estate documents retrieved from safe/attorney.
- [ ] **Certified Death Certificates:** Order 10–15 certified copies (vital for insurance, banks, deeds, social security).
- [ ] **Legal Minor Guardianship:** Formally filed with probate court as designated in Living Trust.
- [ ] **Social Security Survivor Benefits:** Appointment scheduled with local SSA office for surviving spouse and minor children.
- [ ] **Investment & Retirement Transfer:** 401(k), IRA, and taxable brokerage beneficiary claim forms submitted.
- [ ] **Home Deeds & Vehicle Titles:** Transferred to surviving spouse or designated trust.

---

## 🏡 CRITICAL HOME INFRASTRUCTURE AUDIT
- [ ] **Main Water Valve:** Location tested (quarter-turn yellow brass lever).
- [ ] **Main Electrical Breaker:** 200A main disconnect identified in garage or basement.
- [ ] **Gas Meter Shutoff:** Wrench kept adjacent to the outside meter.
- [ ] **Sump Pump & Backup Battery:** Tested annually before rainy season.
- [ ] **HVAC Filter Size & Location:** Documented in Ark Home Specs.

---

## 👵 AGING PARENT SAFEGUARDS (If Applicable)
- [ ] **Durable Power of Attorney (DPOA):** Signed medical and financial copies in safe.
- [ ] **Medicare Cards & Supplemental Insurance:** Policy and group numbers logged.
- [ ] **Primary Cardiologist & Specialist Contacts:** Stored in Ark Sandwich Node.
- [ ] **Credit Bureau Freeze:** Equifax, Experian, TransUnion frozen against identity theft.
