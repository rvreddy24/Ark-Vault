# Commercial validation and draft positioning

Updated 15 November 2026. Consolidates the older launch blueprint and marketplace listing kit. This is research and a test plan, not a forecast or a storefront launch.

## Honest offer

**Ark — Offline Household Handover & Printable Family Organizer**

Make important household information easier for a trusted person to find when you are unavailable. Seven guided essentials, reviewed household notes, selected family/caregiver packets, and encrypted portable backup files. No account, subscription, analytics, remote scripts, or cloud sync. The working browser copy and recovery snapshot are unencrypted. Backups are manual; device compromise and forgotten passphrases are not recoverable by Ark.

Works in current desktop browsers. Standalone HTML requires no Python, but file storage behavior varies. The local-server launcher needs Python 3.9+. Extract the entire download, keep the folder and browser profile stable, and test a backup. There is no mobile app or automatic synchronization. Ark is an organizer, not a password manager, legal-document generator, medical service, or source of authority to act.

## Evidence and competition

- [Pew's 2025 survey](https://www.pewresearch.org/social-trends/2025/11/06/experiences-with-estate-planning-and-discussing-end-of-life-preferences/) supports a preparation gap, not willingness to buy Ark.
- [Colado's comparable offline binder](https://coladosystems.com/systems/emergency-home-binder-pro) was $24.99 once when checked; [beizeiten](https://beizeiten.app/en/) also advertises local storage and encrypted exports. Offline delivery, printing, and one-time pricing are not unique.
- [CFPB/FEMA resources](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/disasters-and-emergencies/get-prepared-before-disaster-emergency-strikes/) provide trusted free guidance. Ark must demonstrate better completion and handover usefulness.
- [Nokbox partnerships](https://www.thenokbox.com/pages/partnerships) demonstrate an existing professional distribution model; they do not prove Ark has professional buyers.

## Test the business

1. Interview 15–20 households across two concrete triggers: upcoming surgery, caregiving transition, frequent partner travel, or recently completed estate planning.
2. Observe 8–10 household pairs. Measure completion, finding five useful answers, printing, and restoration without coaching. Do not collect real credentials.
3. Test actual purchases at $29/$39/$49 after accurate compatibility/security disclosures and seller terms. Record qualified traffic, refunds, support time, completion, and independent recipient use.
4. Seek small paid pilots from professional organizers or care educators. Require evidence of repeat demand before projecting wholesale revenue.
5. Stop or reposition if buyers do not finish, recipients cannot use the handover, substitutes suffice, or acquisition/support consume the margin.

These sample sizes and gates are proposed directional tests, not statistical proof or industry benchmarks. No customer interviews, paid conversion, organic traffic, or sales evidence has been collected by the software work.

## Unit economics are assumptions

At $39, [Gumroad's detailed fee page](https://gumroad.com/help/article/66-gumroads-fees) implied about $33.17 after direct card fees when checked. [Lemon Squeezy's base fees](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/fees) left $36.55 before applicable extras. Assuming a 5% reserve, $5 support allowance, and $10 acquisition leaves about $16.22 per Gumroad direct sale before fixed costs and ongoing work. Three hundred sales would yield about $4,866 contribution, not $11,700 profit. At an assumed 2% conversion, 300 sales need 15,000 qualified visits. None of these traffic, cost, or conversion assumptions is observed Ark performance.

Recheck fees, seller-country eligibility, tax/payment arrangements, and current marketplace rules. [Etsy's October 2026 policy](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1275449912004) prohibits AI-generated functional instructions; establish applicability and content provenance before listing. Its mobile app cannot directly download purchased files. There are no tested search tags in this project.

## Claims to avoid

Do not promise guaranteed protection, audited security, a complete setup in 20 minutes, expected organic sales, a universal legal binder, automatic emergency access, revocable copied briefings, or zero risk. Do not imply cloud competitors all cost $300/year or every physical alternative costs $150+.

Before charging buyers, the seller must publish appropriate purchase/refund terms and delivery/support details for the chosen storefront. Software tests do not establish legal rights, market demand, platform eligibility, or buyer outcomes. See the root release checklist for recorded software evidence and remaining human/hardware decisions.
