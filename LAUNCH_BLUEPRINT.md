# Ark commercial validation plan

Status: product hypothesis, not a validated revenue forecast. Updated 15 November 2026 after repository and market research.

## Offer to investigate

A private household handover that a trusted person can actually use. Target a concrete trigger first: upcoming surgery, a caregiving transition, frequent partner travel, or recently completed estate planning. Test two segments before broadening. The existing Medicare/US estate positioning was not evidence of an international market; the new core prompts avoid prescribing jurisdiction-specific legal actions.

## What is and is not proven

Research supports a preparation gap, not willingness to pay for Ark. Similar offline organizers already exist. One-time pricing, printable packets, and local storage are not unique. No customer interviews, paid conversion, organic traffic, or sales evidence has been collected. Do not claim guaranteed protection, a 20-minute complete audit, expected Etsy volume, or guaranteed B2B revenue.

## Validation gates (proposed, not industry benchmarks)

1. Interview 15–20 households across two trigger-based segments about actual incidents and existing solutions.
2. Observe 8–10 household pairs with fictional data. Measure entry, review, print, restore, and independent retrieval by the intended person.
3. After necessary professional content/security review, test actual purchases at $29/$39/$49. Record qualified traffic, support minutes, refunds, completion, and recipient access. Avoid collecting secrets.
4. Interview 5–10 professional organizers, care educators, or estate-planning partners. Seek small paid pilots and repeat purchases before projecting bulk sales.
5. Scale only when observed contribution after fees, support, refunds, and acquisition is positive. Reposition if people buy but do not finish, recipients cannot access it, or free substitutes are sufficient.

## Economics to validate

At $39, Gumroad's detailed fee page currently implies approximately $33.17 after direct card fees; Lemon Squeezy's base leaves $36.55, before applicable extras. These are proceeds, not profit. Assuming a 5% gross-price reserve, $5 support allowance, and $10 acquisition cost leaves approximately $16.22 per Gumroad sale. Three hundred sales would yield about $4,866 contribution before fixed costs and ongoing work. At an assumed 2% conversion, they require 15,000 qualified visits. Neither assumption is proven.

Sources: [Gumroad fees](https://gumroad.com/help/article/66-gumroads-fees), [Lemon Squeezy fees](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/fees). Recheck current fees, seller eligibility, taxes, and country-specific payments before launch.

## Channel cautions

Etsy is not guaranteed distribution. Its October 2026 policy prohibits AI-generated functional instructions; establish authorship and eligibility before selling instructional content. Its mobile app cannot directly download digital purchases. No tags in this repository have been tested. Professional partnerships are competitive; [Nokbox already offers them](https://www.thenokbox.com/pages/partnerships).

Sources: [Etsy AI policy](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1275449912004), [digital downloads](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115013328108-How-to-Download-a-Digital-Item).
